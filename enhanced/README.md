# 世界风格太刀仿崛起增强版

**Rise-Inspired Enhanced World-Style Long Sword for MHGU**

[中文](#中文) · [English](#english)

## 中文

在《怪物猎人 GU》的独立世界风格上加入《崛起》式派生，保留登龙兜割和世界风格的音效、刀线。

- 大居合反击成功可接看破斩、特殊纳刀或气刃连段。反击失败保留刃色。大居合后的 Y 收刀使用《崛起》330 号动作，播放速度提高 40%。
- 看破成功并且回身刀命中后，R 接大回旋，Y 接无双气刃斩。无双收招可接大回旋、特殊纳刀或同一套 Y 收刀。地面狩技生效后可以预输入 R+B 接特殊纳刀，樱花气刃斩末段可提前截断收刀。
- 气刃突刺为原来的 26 动作值，播放速度提高 40%。命中只扣血，不累计怯硬或断尾值。白刃以上仍可起跳，兜割七段照常结算。

### 下载与安装

适用 MHGU 1.4.0。已用 Windows Recomp 0.18.1 和 Forge 2.0.0 离线副本测试。手机版、模拟器、主机和联机未验证。

1. 下载[独立安装包](https://github.com/Akumi-xu/MHW-Long-Sword-Moveset-for-MHGU/releases/download/v1.2-Rise-Enhanced-Recomp/MHGU-World-Long-Sword-Rise-Enhanced_v1.2-Recomp.zip)。
2. 退出游戏，备份此前的太刀动作 MOD，将包内的 `romfs` 解压至 `mods/MHGU_WORLD_STYLE/`。标准版和增强版共用安装目录，启用其中一份。
3. 重启后在装备箱选太刀的「世界风格」。卸载前先换回原生风格，再退出游戏并停用 MOD 或恢复备份。

实测气刃突刺打中雌火龙尾巴扣 24 HP，怯硬和断尾耐久均保持 1。白、黄、红刃均验证登龙衔接、七段扣血和降刃。红刃及雌火龙样本与显伤插件同开。

## English

Adds Rise-inspired follow-ups to the standalone World Style in Monster Hunter Generations Ultimate, with Helm Breaker and World-style sound and slash effects.

- A successful Iai Spirit Slash counter can lead into Foresight Slash, Special Sheathe, or the Spirit combo. A missed counter retains the Spirit level. Y after Iai Spirit Slash uses Rise motion 330 at 1.4× speed.
- After a successful Foresight Slash and a landed return slash, R leads to Spirit Roundslash and Y to Spirit Reckoning. Spirit Reckoning can lead into Roundslash, Special Sheathe, or the same Y sheathe. Buffer R+B after a grounded Hunter Art takes effect to enter Special Sheathe, including an early cancel of Sakura Slash's final sheathe.
- Spirit Thrust uses its original motion value of 26 and plays at 1.4× speed. It deals HP damage without building flinch or tail-sever damage. The vault still works from white Spirit level, and Helm Breaker's seven hits retain normal damage handling.

### Download and install

Requires MHGU 1.4.0. Tested offline on a separate save with Windows Recomp 0.18.1 and Forge 2.0.0. Mobile Recomp, emulators, console and multiplayer are untested.

1. Download the [standalone package](https://github.com/Akumi-xu/MHW-Long-Sword-Moveset-for-MHGU/releases/download/v1.2-Rise-Enhanced-Recomp/MHGU-World-Long-Sword-Rise-Enhanced_v1.2-Recomp.zip).
2. Close the game and back up the previous moveset mod. Extract the package's `romfs` folder into `mods/MHGU_WORLD_STYLE/`. Both editions use the same mod directory. Enable one edition.
3. Restart and select **World Style** for Long Sword at the equipment box. Before uninstalling, switch to a native style, exit the game, and disable the mod or restore your backup.

In the Rathian test, Spirit Thrust dealt 24 HP to the tail, while both the flinch and sever counters remained at 1. White, yellow, and red Spirit levels each passed the vault, seven-hit damage, and Spirit-level decrease checks. The red-level and Rathian tests also ran alongside the damage display plugin.
